ABOUT

Two months in, hooked on infrastructure.

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate who recently wrapped up a three-month DevOps internship at Pakistan Single Window, where I got hands-on with the tools that take an application from a repo to a running, monitored service in production.

Along the way I picked up GitLab and Jenkins for CI/CD, Kubernetes and ArgoCD for container orchestration and GitOps-style deployment, Prometheus and Grafana for monitoring, some Terraform for infrastructure as code, and RabbitMQ for messaging between services.

Outside of DevOps, I build cross-platform apps with Flutter, and I'm comfortable working across Linux systems and basic networking — the layer everything above ends up running on.
